# Yoohoo
Tired of missing guild chat while you’re busy fighting bosses, running dungeons, or wondering how your bags filled up again?

Yoohoo gives you a little audible heads-up when someone writes in guild chat, so you can join the conversation without keeping one eye on the chat window.

## A little sound, a little less missed
- **Hear when your guild is chatting.** Get a sound notification when a guild message arrives.
- **No echoes from yourself.** Your own messages won’t trigger a notification.
- **Sounds customization.** Change the notification sound using World of Warcraft’s built-in sounds.
